## 3-GPU Modal silicon verification (full LLM 3-op forward layer)

## Honest open gaps

We track open items transparently rather than hiding them:
- **FlashInfer B300 FP4-GEMM (`1 / 5`):** Requires two Blackwell FP4 instruction forms (`UTCCP`, `UTCOMMA`) currently queued in our formal factory pipeline.
- **Qwen3.5 packed GDN decode (`b7`):** Timing tracks physical H200 closely (`5.16 µs` vs. `5.24 µs`), while output buffer `b7` currently diverges at byte offset `3407940`.
- **Large 132-CTA dense grids:** Single-CTA / small-grid evaluations complete in `200–785 ms` cold, while full 132-CTA dense WGMMA grids take `~21 s` cold (`< 1 ms` on deterministic receipt cache hit).
